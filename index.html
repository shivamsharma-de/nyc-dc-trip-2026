Not included in bookings

December weather

Expect about 0 to 7 °C in New York and slightly milder in DC, wind on the coast. Sunset ~16:30 NYC, ~16:45 DC. Pack a warm layer, hat and gloves.

Sort before you fly

  • The flight booking shows 1 passenger. Book or confirm the second traveller's ticket.
  • Indian passport: check that both of you hold a valid US B1/B2 visa (ESTA does not apply).
  • Fix Airbnb dates (DC from 5 Dec, Brooklyn from 9 Dec) and tell the DC host you'll arrive around 01:30–02:00. Ask for self check-in.
  • Add the second traveller to the Avis rental as a driver so you can swap at breaks.
  • Reserve free timed passes for the National Museum of African American History and Culture (same-day passes drop online at 6:30 ET) and the Air and Space Museum.
  • Ask Avis about the toll transponder fee and bring your licence (an International Driving Permit is a cheap safety net).
  • Set up one contactless card or phone wallet per person for NYC subway (OMNY) and DC Metro taps.
Where you're staying

Two bases, both on the subway

Nights 9, 10, 11 Dec

Atkins Ave, Brooklyn

Vibe
Quiet, residential East Brooklyn: brick row houses, Latino and Caribbean bakeries and corner shops, elevated J train rattling over Fulton St. Not touristy, which is what keeps it affordable.
Transit
J Z on the elevated line above Fulton St (Cleveland St / Norwood Av / Crescent St) and A C along Pitkin Ave (Euclid Av is the express stop). Lower Manhattan is about 40–50 min. Tap the same contactless card or phone each ride; fares cap after a week of rides.
To JFK
Take an A marked Far Rockaway or Rockaway Park (not Lefferts Blvd) to Howard Beach, then the AirTrain. Driving takes 20–30 min.
Nature nearby
Highland Park and the Ridgewood Reservoir sit just north of Jamaica Ave: wooded paths and city views. Jamaica Bay Wildlife Refuge is a straight A-train ride (Day 8).
Veg food
Liberty Ave in Ozone Park / Richmond Hill, a few A stops east, is Little Guyana: doubles and channa roti at Singh's Roti Shop (131-18 Liberty Ave). Otherwise cook at home.
Groceries
SuperFresh, 3260 Fulton St (opened Oct 2025). Bigger shop: Gateway Center (501 Gateway Dr) with Aldi, Lidl and ShopRite. Easiest by car on the evening of Day 5.
Nights 5, 6, 7, 8 Dec

Decatur St NW, Washington

Vibe
Leafy rowhouse streets with porches, a mix of long-time residents and newcomers. Georgia Ave has Ethiopian cafés, carry-outs and coffee shops. Calm at night.
Transit
Green Yellow at Georgia Ave–Petworth (3700 Georgia Ave). Archives and L'Enfant Plaza on the Mall are a direct ride, about 15 min. Tap a contactless card to ride. Leave the car parked and read the street signs for permit zones and street-sweeping.
Nature nearby
Rock Creek Park is on your doorstep: a 1,700-acre wooded valley with trails, a creek and Peirce Mill.
Veg food
Ice 'N' Slice (3937 Georgia Ave) does a vegetarian Ethiopian "Addis Ababa" pizza with lentils, cabbage and spinach. Heat Da Spot Café (3213 Georgia Ave) for Ethiopian breakfast and chai. Hedzole (5505 Colorado Ave) does vegan jollof bowls. Büna Coffeehouse (4400 Georgia Ave) for coffee.
Groceries
Safeway, 3830 Georgia Ave NW, steps from the Metro. Target at DC USA in Columbia Heights (one Metro stop south) has a grocery section.
Heads-up
NuVegan Café, Shouk and HipCityVeg, often listed online, have closed.
Day 1 · Sat 5 Dec

Land at JFK, drive through the night to DC

≈370 km · 4–5 h + breaks
  1. 19:05
    Land at JFK Terminal 1

    Allow about an hour for immigration and customs.

  2. 20:15
    AirTrain to Federal Circle → Avis

    The AirTrain between the terminals and the rental station is free. Set up navigation and check the toll transponder before leaving the lot.

  3. 20:45
    Drive out of New York

    Belt Parkway → Verrazzano Bridge → Staten Island → Goethals Bridge → NJ Turnpike south. This skips Manhattan entirely.

  4. 22:00
    Break 1: NJ Turnpike service area

    About 75 min in. Coffee, toilets, fuel, and dinner if you didn't eat on the plane. Swap drivers.

  5. 23:15
    Break 2: Delaware Welcome Center on I-95

    Just after the Delaware Memorial Bridge. Travel plaza with food court and fuel.

  6. 00:15
    Break 3: Maryland House on I-95

    Last stop before Baltimore. Walk around for 15 minutes, swap drivers again.

  7. 01:30
    Arrive at Decatur St NW

    Through Baltimore on I-95, then the Baltimore–Washington Parkway into DC. Park on the street and read the signs for permit and street-sweeping rules.

Driving safely after a long flight

  • Midnight in DC is 06:00 in Germany, so you'll have been awake about 22 hours by the end. Stop every 60–90 minutes, even if you feel fine.
  • Swap drivers at each break; the second traveller must be on the rental agreement.
  • If either of you feels drowsy, pull into a service area and nap for 20 minutes rather than pushing through.
  • Speed limits are mostly 55–65 mph (88–105 km/h).

Tolls and fuel

Expect tolls on the Verrazzano Bridge, NJ Turnpike, Delaware I-95, Maryland I-95 and the Fort McHenry tunnel; the rental's transponder pays them automatically. Fill up in New Jersey or Delaware, where fuel is cheaper.

Eat (veg)

Service plazas have pizza, pretzels and sandwiches with vegetarian options. Pack a few snacks in your hand luggage too.

Day 2 · Sun 6 Dec

Sleep in, groceries, monuments at dusk

Metro · all free
  1. 10:30
    Sleep in

    You arrived around 01:30. Let the jet lag settle.

  2. 11:30
    Groceries at Safeway, 3830 Georgia Ave

    Breakfast, lunch supplies and staples for three days of cooking.

  3. 13:00
    Short walk in Rock Creek Park

    Daylight and fresh air help reset your body clock.

  4. 14:45
    Metro to the Mall

    Green or Yellow to Archives or L'Enfant Plaza.

  5. 15:00
    Washington Monument → WWII Memorial → Reflecting Pool

    A flat 2 km walk west along the Mall.

  6. 16:30
    Lincoln Memorial at sunset

    Climb the steps, then look back east down the Reflecting Pool as the Monument lights up.

  7. 17:15
    MLK Memorial and the Tidal Basin

    Then the National Christmas Tree on the Ellipse near the White House, usually lit in early December.

The vibe

The Mall at dusk is the best free show in DC: white marble going gold, then floodlit, with far fewer people than in daytime.

What the memorials are

  • Lincoln Memorial: Greek-temple tribute to Abraham Lincoln, where Martin Luther King Jr. gave "I Have a Dream" in 1963.
  • WWII Memorial: 56 pillars and two arches for the states and the Atlantic and Pacific theatres.
  • MLK Memorial: King carved emerging from a "Stone of Hope".

Eat (veg)

Lunch at home from the Safeway shop. Dinner back on Georgia Ave: vegetarian Addis Ababa pizza at Ice 'N' Slice, or cook with Safeway groceries.

Must do

Be at the Lincoln Memorial steps by 16:30.

Day 3 · Mon 7 Dec

Smithsonian day

All free · Metro only
  1. 09:30
    Metro to Smithsonian or Archives

    Museums open around 10:00.

  2. 10:00
    National Museum of Natural History

    Hope Diamond, the Hall of Fossils with T. rex, the Ocean Hall's whale model, Hall of Human Origins. 2 hours.

  3. 12:15
    Lunch at Mitsitam Café

    Inside the National Museum of the American Indian; Native dishes from across the Americas with vegetarian choices.

  4. 13:30
    National Air and Space Museum

    The Wright Flyer, Apollo 11 command module, Spirit of St. Louis. Free timed pass.

  5. 15:30
    National Museum of African American History and Culture

    Start at the bottom: the history galleries rise from the slave trade to the present. Needs at least 2 hours; open until 17:30.

  6. 18:00
    Evening

    Walk past the Capitol, lit at night, then Metro home.

What the museums are about

  • Natural History: 145+ million specimens; dinosaurs, gems, mammals, human evolution.
  • Air and Space: the world's most-visited aviation museum, from the first powered flight to the Moon.
  • NMAAHC: the bronze-latticed building telling American history through Black experience; emotional, essential, popular.

Passes

NMAAHC: same-day online passes from 6:30 ET; weekday walk-ups are limited, from 13:00 on Madison Dr. Air and Space: reserve free timed entry online.

Eat (veg)

Mitsitam for lunch. Dinner in Adams Morgan: shiro (spiced chickpea stew) on injera at Tsehay, an easy bus ride from Petworth.

Must do

The NMAAHC history galleries. Book the pass the moment it opens.

  7. 18:00

The Main Reading Room overlook in the Jefferson Building.

Day 5 · Wed 9 Dec

Wild ponies on Assateague, then back to Brooklyn

≈3 h + ≈5 h driving
  1. 07:00
    Check out, drive east

    US-50 over the Chesapeake Bay Bridge, across the flat farm country of Maryland's Eastern Shore.

  2. 10:00
    Assateague Island Visitor Center

    Open 9:00–16:00 daily. Small aquarium tanks and exhibits on the barrier island.

  3. 10:30
    Into the Maryland district

    Buy a vehicle pass at the entrance station (valid 7 days). Drive the bayside road slowly; the wild horses often graze by the roadside.

  4. 11:00
    Three short nature trails

    Life of the Marsh, Life of the Forest and Life of the Dunes, each under a mile on boardwalk.

  5. 12:30
    Picnic on an empty winter beach

    Windy, wide and nearly deserted in December.

  6. 14:00
    Drive north to Brooklyn

    Via Delaware, the Delaware Memorial Bridge and the NJ Turnpike, about 5 h with a stop.

  7. 19:30
    Check in on Atkins Ave, return the car

    Either tonight at JFK (then AirTrain + A home) or tomorrow morning on the way into Manhattan.

What's in the nature

A 37-mile barrier island of dunes, pine forest and salt marsh. The Maryland side's horses descend from animals living here for centuries. In winter you'll also see migrating ducks, geese and herons in the marsh.

Rules

The horses are wild and bite and kick. Stay at least 40 feet (a bus length) away and never feed them.

Eat

Off-season, most nearby restaurants in Ocean City are closed. Bring a packed lunch and a thermos.

Too much driving?

Eight hours at the wheel is a lot. Alternative: skip Assateague and drive DC → Brooklyn directly (4–5 h), or do Assateague as Tuesday's round trip from DC.

Must do

See the wild horses, then walk over the dunes to the Atlantic.

Day 7 · Fri 11 Dec

The Met, Central Park and holiday Midtown

Subway
  1. 09:00
    Subway uptown

    A to 59 St–Columbus Circle, then walk through the park.

  2. 09:30
    Central Park walk

    The Mall's elm avenue, Bethesda Terrace, Bow Bridge, then east to Fifth Ave.

  3. 10:30
    The Metropolitan Museum of Art

    Open until 21:00 on Fridays. Pick 3 or 4 wings: Temple of Dendur, Arms and Armor, European paintings, the rooftop if open.

  4. 14:00
    Lunch on the Upper East Side

    Ginger Root on 1st Ave, a 10-min walk from the Met.

  5. 15:30
    Down Fifth Avenue

    Holiday window displays, St. Patrick's Cathedral.

  6. 16:30
    Rockefeller Center tree

    Lit on 2 Dec 2026. Skaters on the rink below. Busy; go early in the evening.

  7. 17:30
    Grand Central and Bryant Park

    The celestial ceiling and Whispering Gallery, then the free Winter Village holiday shops in Bryant Park.

  8. 19:30
    Times Square, briefly

    Ten minutes is enough. A or C from 42 St home.

What the Met is about

5,000 years of art from every continent under one roof: an Egyptian temple, Greek and Roman sculpture, medieval armour, Vermeer and Van Gogh. It's too big for one visit, so choose your wings and skip the rest.

Eat (veg)

  • Ginger Root, 1164 1st Ave: vegan Chinese, near the Met.
  • Vegan on the Fly, 24 W 45th St: cheap vegan fast food in Midtown.
  • PLNT Burger near Bryant Park: cheap plant burgers.
  • HanGawi, 2 E 32nd St: serene vegan Korean if you want one splurge.

Swap if you prefer

Instead of Times Square: the High Line, Chelsea Market and Hudson Yards in the evening.

Must do

The Temple of Dendur at the Met, and the Rockefeller tree after dark.

What's in the nature

Salt marsh, ponds and dunes inside city limits, with the Manhattan skyline on the horizon. Winter brings brant geese, buffleheads and other ducks; owls turn up some years. Planes from JFK pass overhead.

Carry-on only

The Light fare usually has no checked bag. Shop accordingly through the week.

Must do

Leave for JFK by 14:30. Weekend subway work happens; check the MTA app the night before.

What to eat

Cheap, vegetarian, and local

All of these are vegetarian or have a standard vegetarian version, and none cost much.

NYC bagel

Everything bagel with scallion cream cheese from any neighbourhood bagel shop. Breakfast for a few dollars.

A plain slice

Thin, foldable, eaten standing up. Cheese or veggie slice.

Doubles

Trinidadian street food: two soft fried breads with curried chickpeas, on Liberty Ave a few stops from your base.

Halal-cart falafel

Falafel over rice with white and hot sauce from a street cart. Cheap, filling, everywhere in Manhattan.

Ethiopian in DC

DC has one of the largest Ethiopian communities outside Africa. Order shiro or a veggie combo on injera.

Dosa cart

NY Dosas in Washington Square Park: cheap vegan dosas and uttapam from a long-running cart.
